About Me

Hi, I’m Raghav Pratap Singh, a Computer Science and Engineering student based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, at Swami Keshvanand Institute of Technology, Management & Gramothan (SKIT).


Some people grow up dreaming of becoming superheroes — I grew up wanting to build like one. From creating automation tools like Postwave and LiNEDATA, to developing a battery safety monitoring system powered by machine learning, I’ve always believed that technology is our real-world superpower.


With skills in Python, C++, Java, and a deep interest in system optimization, I love building solutions that make everyday tasks smarter, faster, and more efficient. My passion lies in automation, AI-driven systems, and pushing machines to their full potential.


I’m always on the lookout for opportunities where I can apply my skills to solve real-world problems, build smarter systems, and — most importantly — keep creating cool stuff.

Why a personal website like this?

Because nostalgia hits different.

Windows XP was the first operating system I ever used, and it left a lasting impact. So, I challenged myself to recreate that classic environment — giving you the feeling of browsing through my computer, just like the good old days.
